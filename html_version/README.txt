Capital Monopoly

Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari).
Keep the audio folder next to the HTML file.

Modes:
- Play with bots — 2 to 8 players, eight bot personalities.
- Multiplayer — create a room and share its 6-character code, or join by code.

Controls:
- Space or R — roll the dice
- P — pause / resume (the game also pauses when the tab is hidden)
- Esc — close a property window (trade offers must be accepted/declined)
- Click a property on the board, or its colored chip in the Players panel,
  to build, sell or mortgage.

Trading:
- With bots — open Trade on your turn before rolling.
- Multiplayer — open Trade on your turn to offer properties + cash
  to another player. Host approves, target accepts/declines.

View:
- 2D / 3D button in the top bar switches renderers. Same game state,
  same rules, same turn system, same multiplayer sync in both modes.
- 3D needs internet once to load three.js (CDN). If it fails,
  the game stays in 2D.
- 3D camera: drag to rotate, wheel / pinch to zoom, click a tile
  to open the same property window as in 2D.

Saving:
- Single-player games save automatically at the start of every turn
  (and when you press Save). Use "Continue game" on the main menu.
- Multiplayer hides Save. Refresh-safe: same tab rejoins the room
  automatically, host restores the last state.

Audio:
- audio/capital-theme.mp3 — background loop
- audio/*.wav — dice, movement and game sound effects
